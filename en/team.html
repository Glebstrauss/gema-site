Research

Research Lines

Four research lines guide GEMA's investigations, combining quantitative, molecular and conservation methods applied to domestic animals.

01
Biostatistics Applied to Genomics

Development and application of biostatistical methods for genomic data analysis and animal evaluation, including mixed models and genomic selection.

02
Molecular Genetics Applied to Breeding

Use of molecular markers, SNP analysis and genomic tools to support animal breeding programs in breeds of zootechnical interest.

03
Quantitative Genetics and Genomics

Estimation of genetic parameters, prediction of breeding values (BLUP/BLUPF90+) and assessment of genetic progress in domestic animal populations.

04
Conservation Genetics

Analysis of genetic diversity, population structure, inbreeding and conservation strategies for locally adapted domestic animal breeds.
